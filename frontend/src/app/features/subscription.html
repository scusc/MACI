Verified Membership

Rally is a network based on verified identities, not anonymous profiles.

  • Why verify? To ensure every member in a travel pool or meetup is a real person.
  • Is my data safe? We use Stripe Identity for military-grade document verification. Rally does not store your ID.
  • What happens after? You unlock the ability to join pools, host meetups, and commit funds.
